## WHAT IS ATLAX?

ATLAX is a market-intelligence service that delivers structured, explainable
trading signals and market context through Telegram. It helps subscribers
follow a chosen style and review information before making their own decisions.

## HOW DOES ATLAX WORK?

ATLAX watches the configured markets, waits for qualifying opportunities, and
sends alerts that match your selected style, timeframes, pairs, and settings.
You remain responsible for reviewing every alert and deciding what to do.

## WHAT DO SUBSCRIBERS RECEIVE?

Subscribers receive Telegram alerts for their selected style and pairs. An
alert may include the pair, timeframe, direction, price levels, confidence,
status, and a short explanation when the information is available.

## DOES ATLAX MANAGE MY TRADING ACCOUNT?

No. ATLAX does not connect to, control, or manage a subscriber's broker account,
wallet, balance, positions, profit, or loss.

## DOES ATLAX PLACE TRADES FOR SUBSCRIBERS?

No. Subscriber access is for signal delivery and market information. ATLAX
does not place, change, close, or manage trades on a subscriber's account.

## WHICH TRADING STYLES ARE AVAILABLE?

Scalper receives short-term alerts. Day Trader receives intraday alerts. Swing
Trader receives broader market alerts. All Styles receives alerts from all
configured subscriber timeframes. You can change your style later in settings.

## CAN I CHOOSE WHICH PAIRS I RECEIVE?

Yes. Open signal settings and choose the pairs you want to follow. Leaving the
pair selection empty means you receive alerts for all available configured
pairs.

## WHAT IS PREMIUM CONFLUENCE?

Premium confluence is the strictest subscriber filter. An alert must show both
clear setup quality and an important market area visible on a broader view.
This option is designed for subscribers who prefer fewer, more selective
alerts.

## WHAT ARE INDEPENDENT FILTERS?

Independent filters let you choose the quality filter and the major-price-area
filter separately. You can receive all valid alerts, only stronger alerts, only
alerts near major price areas, or use both filters together.

## WHAT DOES HIGH-QUALITY ONLY MEAN?

High-quality only means ATLAX applies stricter checks designed to remove weak
or noisy alerts and favor clearer opportunities with stronger evidence. It
does not guarantee a winning result.

## WHY HAVE I NOT RECEIVED A SIGNAL?

There may be no qualifying opportunity for your selected style or pairs, the
market may be quiet or closed, your filters may be strict, or market data may
be temporarily unavailable. ATLAX does not create alerts just to fill the
feed.

## WHAT DOES A SIGNAL MEAN?

A signal is an advisory market alert based on ATLAX's documented analysis
process. It is information for independent review, not a promise, instruction,
or guarantee of a result.

## DOES A CONFIDENCE SCORE MEAN A TRADE WILL WIN?

No. A confidence score describes the strength of the available evidence. It is
not a guaranteed probability of profit and it cannot remove market risk.

## HOW DO I SUBSCRIBE?

Use `/subscribe` and choose an available payment method. ATLAX creates a
payment reference for your request. After payment, submit the transaction
reference with `/paid` and wait for verification before access is activated.

## WHICH PAYMENT METHODS ARE AVAILABLE?

ATLAX shows the available payment methods in the subscription menu. These may
include USDC on the Solana network and a mobile-money payment option. Always
follow the payment details and reference shown in your own checkout.

## HOW DO I PAY WITH M-PESA?

Open the M-Pesa option in `/subscribe` and follow the displayed instructions.
Use the ATLAX payment number and amount shown there, keep your confirmation
message, and submit only the requested payment reference for verification.

## WHAT HAPPENS AFTER I SUBMIT PAYMENT PROOF?

Your payment request enters verification. An authorized ATLAX administrator
checks the reference and activates access after the payment is confirmed. Use
`/status` to review the latest state.

## CAN I RENEW BEFORE MY ACCESS EXPIRES?

Yes. Use `/renew` or open the subscription menu. When access is still active,
the renewal extends the current period instead of removing your existing time.

## CAN I USE ATLAX AI?

Active subscribers can ask ATLAX AI about market news, heatmap context, pair
relationships, signal explanations, public ATLAX results, and general trading
education. ATLAX AI is advisory and subject to a fair-use daily limit.

## WHAT CAN ATLAX AI NOT DO?

ATLAX AI cannot access or manage your account, place trades, reveal protected
system details, guarantee an outcome, or replace your own risk decisions.

## ARE ATLAX RESULTS GUARANTEED?

No. Markets are uncertain and past results do not guarantee future results.
ATLAX provides information for independent review. Use a personal risk plan
and seek qualified advice when appropriate.

## HOW DO I CHANGE MY SETTINGS?

Use `/settings` to update pairs, notifications, confidence, quality filters,
and the premium confluence option. Use `/style` to change your trading style.

## HOW DO I CONTACT SUPPORT?

For access, payment, or account questions, contact the ATLAX administrator at
@ocrooxx. Never send a password, payment PIN, wallet seed phrase, or private
key in a support message.
