# ADR-004: External Payment Rails For Telegram Subscriptions

Status: Accepted  
Date: 2026-08-08

## Context

ATLAX sells a digital service: Telegram signal access. The product must support crypto-wallet payment instructions and M-Pesa payment instructions, but it must remain deterministic and auditable.

Telegram's official payments guidance requires Telegram Stars for digital goods sold inside Telegram. ATLAX is not using Telegram invoice payments for this subscription flow because the access is provisioned through external payment confirmation and then activated in ATLAX.

## Decision

ATLAX will use external payment rails for subscription collection:

- USDC wallet instructions for manual or provider-backed crypto payment.
- Vodacom M-Pesa Lipa Namba instructions for manual or provider-backed mobile money payment.

Telegram will be used to present the purchase menu, collect payment references, and confirm access after payment is validated.

Subscription activation will happen only after a payment is confirmed by an approved workflow.

## Consequences

- Telegram Stars are reserved for future in-Telegram digital goods flows.
- Subscription access must be activated by an approved confirmation path.
- Payment intents must be stored and auditable.
- The M-Pesa rail must use an operator-configured merchant Lipa Namba and
  explicit TZS amount; ATLAX must not infer an exchange rate.
- User-facing commands must not self-register access without payment or invite code authority.
- Admin review remains available for manual wallet and M-Pesa confirmation.
