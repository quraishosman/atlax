# ADR-020: Phase 2 Profit-Lock Shadow Observation

Status: Accepted for shadow validation  
Date: 2026-09-19  
Owners: ATLAX Engineering

## Context

ATLAX needs evidence about how often a profitable position gives back gains
before TP1 or TP2. A live exit rule cannot be enabled safely without observing
that behavior against the actual broker fill and original approved stop.

## Decision

Phase 2 records maximum favorable excursion in normalized R units for each
tracked ATLAX position. It evaluates the configured activation, minimum lock,
and maximum giveback thresholds and emits `profit_lock_would_apply` when a
protective lock would be valid at the current market price.

The phase is shadow-only. It must not modify stops, close partial volume, close
the remaining position, or change alert routing. The original stop is captured
at position registration; missing or invalid risk distance produces UNKNOWN
telemetry and no inferred value.

## Consequences

- Restart-safe state includes `original_stop`, `mfe_r`, and the shadow-log flag.
- Logs can be compared with eventual journal outcomes before live promotion.
- Phase 3 closed-candle momentum-loss actions remain a separate approval.
- The configured transaction-cost flag is reserved until a broker-cost adapter
  can provide validated values; Phase 2 does not invent a cost estimate.
